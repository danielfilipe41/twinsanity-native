Crash Twinsanity

This build carries none of the game's data: it reads it out of your own disc at every start. Choose the disc image (an ISO, or a raw dump of the disc); it is read here in the page and never uploaded. Saves and settings are kept in this browser's own storage, so they are there next time. Dropping the image onto the page works too, and so does dropping a folder of mods. An image of another region (the North American or the Japanese disc) plays with the European release's SLES_525.68 given below, which is the program this build's data is read out of.
